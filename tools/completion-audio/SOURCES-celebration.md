# Sampled celebration source credits

Local prototype only; no sounds are integrated into the game yet.

- **Hyper-Ultra-Fanfare / sboe.wav** — Zane Little Music.
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/hyper-ultra-fanfare
  Original download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/sboe.wav
  License: CC0 1.0, confirmed on the source page.
  Edited: 3.42–3.60 s melodic pickup time-compressed to 140 ms without
  transposition, followed by the 3.60–4.70 s ending excerpt. Resampled to
  48 kHz, filtered, subtle reverb added, level adjusted and faded.
- **Short Crowd Cheer 2.flac** — qubodup, recorded at a small concert.
  Source: https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/182572/
  License: CC0 1.0, as stated on the source page.
  Local input: the publicly shared high-quality MP3 rendition from
  https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/182/182572_71257-hq.mp3
  Edited: 1.25–2.05 s cheer excerpt placed at 0.18 s for the roar;
  2.05–4.65 s continuous applause placed at 0.26 s for the longer tail.
  Resampled to 48 kHz, filtered, faded and mixed. The two sections overlap.

License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
