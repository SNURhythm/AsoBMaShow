# Google Translate attribution

`google-translate.png` is the unmodified `png/color-short@2x.png` badge from
Google's [attribution asset archive](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/translate/images/google-translate-attribution.zip).
It is displayed at its 122 × 16 point size on a white background next to Android
translation results. Google brand assets remain owned by Google and are used
under its [translation attribution requirements](https://docs.cloud.google.com/translate/attribution).
